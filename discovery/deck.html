      04 · User jobs & needs

      Jobs are about confidence and continuity.

      Knowing where things stand, trusting the decision, and never repeating yourself to another part of Bloom. Pick a stakeholder.

        Jobs every group shares

        • Know what needs my attention, and what's waiting on someone else.
        • Get to a real person quickly when it matters.
        • Do it once: not re-enter who I am for each part of Bloom.
        • Feel that Bloom knows me, without feeling watched.

          08 · Existing Bloom design language

          Five traits to carry forward.

          1. It greets you.It opens with a person, by name, and what matters to them today.
          2. It edits.It says what needs you most rather than listing everything.
          3. Warm on paper, deep in navy.Off-white editorial calm; navy for focus, electric blue for action.
          4. It is crafted.Type, motion and material do the premium work. Nothing is loud.
          5. You act where you are.Approve, book, wish, copy: no detours.

          11 · Key questions for Phase 2

          Two questions set the scope of everything after them.

          Question 01

          Is the ecosystem for customers, employees, or both, and in what order?

          The approved language was built for employees; customer products carry different stakes.

          Question 02

          Who owns the relationship in each domain: Bloom or the operator?

          Marriott, Rotana and Brighton College own their guests' and students' journeys.

            Summary

            Decide whose ecosystem it is. Then test the household and the community.

            KnownWhat we now know

            1. Four segments (real estate, education, services, hospitality) meet physically in Bloom communities; Bloom Living is the clearest case.
            2. Buyers, owners, residents and school families overlap heavily; the household is the real unit.
            3. The lifecycle runs Discover to Evolve. Arrive is the busiest cross-domain moment; Wait is the least supported.
            4. Customer touchpoints are split by audience and business unit.
            5. Both approved experiences are for employees, and hold the strongest expression of digital Bloom.

            CheckWhat we still need to validate

            1. That the approved apps are BlooMultiverse, and what the Mobile App adds.
            2. The corporate site's visual system, and how it relates to the flag mark and BlooMultiverse colours.
            3. What Bloom Universal is.
            4. Portfolio figures that sources disagree on: schools, students, current projects.
            5. Whether the Community App and Super App share accounts and data.
            6. How often one household holds several Bloom roles.

            NextWhat Phase 2 should investigate

            1. Scope: customers, employees or both, and the sequence.
            2. Research with real households in one community, across the full lifecycle. Bloom Living is the natural candidate.
            3. A service blueprint of Arrive and Wait.
            4. A brand-architecture decision on how Bloom appears beside operator and school brands.
            5. A first-hand audit of every touchpoint against the principles.
            6. The data and identity foundations, before any AI features.
