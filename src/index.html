08Support

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about finding your way around BlooMultiverse.

What is BlooMultiverse?

BlooMultiverse is Bloom’s employee portal. Your tasks, apps, policies, announcements and perks are in one place, so you don’t have to switch between systems.

How do I access my profile?

Select your name in the top-right corner, then choose My profile. You’ll see your role, team and contact details.

How do I update my personal information?

Personal details are managed in Darwinbox. Open Darwinbox from Quick links, go to your profile and submit the change. Some changes need HR approval before they appear.

How do I search for information?

Use the search bar at the top of any page, or press Ctrl K. You can find people, policies and apps from one place.

Where can I find company policies?

Go to Explore policies on the home page. Filter by category, or select Browse all policies to see the full library.

How can I view or download a policy?

Open any policy to read it in your browser. Use the download option on the policy page to save a PDF copy.

Where can I find company guidelines?

Guidelines, such as the Brand Guidelines, are listed with the policies in Explore policies. You can also search for a guideline by name.

Where can I see company announcements?

The latest announcements appear on the home page, right after Welcome to the Bloom family. Use the arrows to move between them.

How can I wish a colleague on their birthday?

Birthdays appear in Announcements. Select Send birthday wish, pick or write a message, then select Send wish.

Still need a hand?Our support team can help with anything in BlooMultiverse.
